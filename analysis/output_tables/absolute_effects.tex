\textbf{Notes:} HR/ARD/NNT are based on the answer-arrival increment ($\beta_4$), the primary (headline) estimand. \emph{Control rate} is the mean number of helping events per observation window among the matched \emph{control} (unanswered) questions in each row's universe --- i.e.\ a recurrent-event \emph{rate}, not a probability or risk. The absolute effect is obtained as $\mathrm{ARD} = \text{control rate}\times(\mathrm{HR}-1)$, which treats the arrival hazard ratio as a rate (risk) ratio; with a base rate near $0.13$ and a recurrent outcome this is a first-order approximation rather than an exact risk difference. \emph{Events/1k} $= 1000\times\mathrm{ARD}$ is the implied number of additional helping events per $1{,}000$ answered questions. Because the outcome is a recurrent-event rate (not a binary risk), $\mathrm{NNT}=1/\mathrm{ARD}$ is an approximate ``answers needed per additional helping event'' rather than a true number-needed-to-treat.
